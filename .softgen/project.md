## Vision
Aruba Royalty Stamp - A production-ready, multi-tenant digital loyalty stamp SaaS for Aruba businesses.
Allows businesses to issue digital stamps via QR codes to customers, who earn rewards when thresholds are met.
Built for scalability across Caribbean markets, featuring strict data isolation, real transactions, and immutable stamp history.

## Design
- **Direction:** Caribbean Utility (Tropical pragmatism with modern clarity)
- **Primary Accent:** Coral/Flamingo `--primary: 350 80% 60% (vibrant coral)`
- **Background:** Sun-washed cream `--background: 40 33% 98%`
- **Text/Data:** Ocean-slate `--foreground: 215 25% 20%`
- **Typography:** Headings: *Sora* (Google Fonts), Body: *Rubik* (Google Fonts)
- **Vibe:** Clean, functional, tabular alignment for data, airy card-based density.

## Features
- Multi-tenant business dashboards
- Immutable stamp transactions
- Dynamic QR code generation & scanning
- Role-based authorization (Super Admin, Owner, Staff, Customer)
- Future-proof payment abstraction layer

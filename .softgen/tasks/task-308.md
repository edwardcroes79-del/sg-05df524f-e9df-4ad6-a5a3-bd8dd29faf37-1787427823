## Notes
Add a Loyalty Card Assignment section to the existing Staff Management area for Business Admins. Admins must be able to select a staff member, view all active loyalty cards in their business, assign multiple cards, remove individual assignments, and review current assignments without unintentionally replacing unrelated ones.

## Checklist
- [ ] Inspect the current staff management page and existing assignment UI patterns
- [ ] Show business staff list and active loyalty cards in the same business
- [ ] Build multi-select or checkbox assignment controls for one or more cards per staff member
- [ ] Save additive assignment changes without unintentionally removing existing ones
- [ ] Allow individual assignment removal and display current assigned cards per staff member
- [ ] Show clear success and error states

## Acceptance
A Business Admin can assign, review, add, and remove multiple loyalty cards for each staff member from Staff Management.
Different staff members can share the same loyalty card.
